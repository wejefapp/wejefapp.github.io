Wejef Terms of Use

Effective October 16, 2026

Wejef is a photo journal that stays on this device. These terms cover the journal you write, the gift packs you buy, and the scripted demo pages included with the app. There is no account.

The journal

You can take or import a photo and record an optional voice note. Only selected photos and recordings you confirm are stored in this app on your device. You can write without media. A page appears in the timeline only after a successful archive. Clearing data removes attached media too.

Gift packs and treasure chests

Archiving a new day uses 1 credit. Looking at the review page and updating the same day use no credits. Each gift pack contains the credit amount shown on its detail page. Your current balance is displayed at the top of the shop. The welcome gift grants 10 credits only when you tap Claim, once per local profile after notification permission is handled. The shop lists only Gift Packs and Treasure Chests at $0.99, $2.99, $9.99, $19.99, $29.99, $49.99, $69.99, and $99.99. Prices are shown in US dollars. Packs are consumable, stay on this device, and are not restored after you remove the app or choose Clear All Data. Only verified, successfully saved transactions add credits. Cancelled, failed and unverified purchases add no credits. Buying credits does not automatically archive a page.

Only verified purchases are accepted. A transaction is finished only after its on-device record is saved. A failed journal save preserves the draft and does not spend credits. Refunded transactions remove only their remaining credits; previously saved pages are retained.

Demo pages

Timeline includes scripted pages marked Demo. They are not your journal and are not for sale.

Permissions
