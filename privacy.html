Wejef Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Wejef keeps your journal on this device. There is no account, no cloud library, and no social graph. This policy describes what this version actually does.

What stays on the device

  • Journal pages you archive: text, mood stamps, weather stamp, place, tags, attached photos, and optional voice notes.
  • Unfinished drafts.
  • Your agreement checkbox, permission choices, and consumable gift-pack records.
  • Scripted Demo pages shipped with the app. They are sample content, not collected from you.

Notifications and tracking

Wejef requests Apple's notification permission directly after onboarding. This version does not send notifications. Wejef does not request tracking permission, read an advertising identifier, or track activity across apps.

Camera, photos, and microphone

Camera, photo-library and microphone permissions are requested only through Apple's system prompts when you tap Take Photo, Import from Photos or Record. There are no custom permission pre-prompts. Only photos you confirm are copied into the app's private storage. Voice notes are recorded only after you tap Record and grant permission; they are saved locally with your entry and can be played or removed. Recording stops when you leave the editor or background the app. Photos and audio are not automatically uploaded or written back to your photo library. Selected Live Photos retain their still-image and paired motion resources inside the app. If you decline a permission, you can still type.

Writing rhythm charts

Yearly and monthly statistics are calculated from your saved journal pages on this device. Demo pages are excluded. The embedded DGCharts library renders charts locally and does not receive or upload your journal.

Purchases

Gift packs and treasure chests are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Wejef stores verified transaction identifiers and the remaining credits on this device to prevent duplicate grants and support journal archiving. Apple processes payment and may collect purchase information under its own privacy policy; Wejef does not receive your payment-card details. The app shows your credit balance in the shop and on the home screen. Pack details show the included credits. Your welcome gift adds 10 credits only after you tap Claim, and the app stores a one-time claim marker. Unverified, cancelled, or failed purchases are not recorded and do not archive a page. Deleting the app or using Clear All Data removes the on-device record. Consumable packs cannot be restored.

Clearing data

Clear All Data deletes journals, drafts, gift-pack records, and the agreement check. Demo pages can appear again because they are part of the app, not your personal journal.

Backups and sharing

Backup & Restore exports a file containing your personal pages, attached photos, Live Photo resources, voice notes, and saved draft. Backups do not contain your credit balance, transaction identifiers, or welcome-gift claim marker. You choose the destination through Files. PDF export uses the system share sheet only after you request it. If you select a cloud or sharing provider, that provider receives the content you choose under its own policies. Wejef does not automatically send backups to a server. Backup files are not encrypted by Wejef; anyone who can access a file can read its contents. Imported files are checked before journal content is changed.

Hosted policy

Opening this policy in the app contacts GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process standard connection information such as your IP address and browser headers. Your journal, photos, and voice notes are not sent with this request.
